# Refund operations — local Phase 7

Event operations includes **Refund policy**. Choose review required, cancellation-only, deadline-based, non-refundable subject to human/legal review, or custom review. Record explicit terms and any deadline/material-change condition; submit for a different administrator's review. Policies are prospective. An approved policy is stamped with its version, reviewer and time. Each new booking snapshots that policy. Existing bookings retain their original policy; unknown legacy terms require human review. Test approval is not legal approval.

Central eligibility requires confirmed organizer-direct money and considers original terms, cancellation/material change, deadline, booking state and used tickets. A used ticket requires support rather than ordinary automatic refund review. A non-refundable label does not settle consumer rights: the software requires an administrator exception review. It cannot override law or invent a real-event policy.

1. Customer opens My bookings → Refund requests and enters their own booking reference and reason. Duplicate requests reuse the existing record.
2. Authorized organizer/admin reviews the original policy and records Under review, Approved or Rejected with notes. Customers/scanner staff/unrelated organizers cannot decide. Approval atomically cancels the booking, revokes active tickets and releases stock once; it never sends money.
3. Move approved requests to Awaiting organizer transfer. In this rehearsal, only fictional transfer references are recorded as Transfer reported.
4. Only the booking owner may separately attest receipt. The historical REFUND_CONFIRMED status denotes that attestation; it is **not independent bank verification**. Original payment amount, recipient/version snapshot, proof and transition history remain intact.
5. Customer/organizer receive deduplicated relevant updates, including customer receipt acknowledgement. Disputes use private Support; receipt acknowledgement is never inferred from a job run.

Cancellation closes new sales and admission immediately, invalidates pending changes and flags confirmed money for refund review. It preserves used-ticket/attendance and financial history. Repeated cancellation is safe. Do not promise real refunds, perform a real bank transfer, or treat a receipt screenshot as independently received funds during local tests.

Remaining: owner-approved actual refund/reschedule rights, consumer/legal review, responsible contacts, review deadlines and real banking procedure. External mail is unconnected.
