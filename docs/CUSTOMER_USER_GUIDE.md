# Customer guide

Phase 11 · October 27, 2026 · fictional localhost use only. Real ticket sales are disabled. This guide describes existing screens; it does not authorize real payment or entry.

1. **Open your event link.** Private invitations are tied to an existing account. Sign in with that account; forwarding the link does not invite another person. An approved public fictional event can offer account registration. Enter your real account details only in a future authorized service, never this fictional rehearsal. Password recovery currently uses a private local test mailbox; real recovery email is not connected.
2. **Find an event.** In the authorized full application, Explore supports search and filters, saved events and a protected map. During Coming Soon, ordinary visitors cannot browse the full application. Your approved event context does not unlock the rest of the site. Exact private venues are withheld until the applicable confirmation rules pass.
3. **Register.** Select the tier, quantity and entry option. Complete the required questions, check the event terms and submit once. The server decides price and availability. Missing questions, expired invitations or unavailable places prevent registration. The actual Halloween draft is not open for booking.
4. **Read your booking.** Open its saved booking page or **My bookings**. Payment review, registration approval and BYOB review are separate. Pending does not mean confirmed. A saved booking reference helps support investigate a lost response; refresh its status before trying again.
5. **Payment instructions.** The panel shows the organizer's approved recipient version and amount saved with this booking. The customer pays the organizer directly in a future authorized event; MixerMates does not hold the money. **Do not transfer money in this rehearsal.** Do not substitute a different QR or historical recipient. If something looks wrong, stop and open Support.
6. **Upload evidence.** Use the requested private payment/BYOB upload. The current customer validator accepts one genuine JPEG/PNG/WebP image up to 10,000,000 bytes; follow the displayed input restrictions. Uploading a screenshot does not confirm incoming funds. The organizer reviews it independently. If correction is requested, submit replacement evidence through the same booking; previous evidence stays in history. Do not make a second payment because issuance is delayed.
7. **Open a ticket.** Once every required review passes, open the digital ticket from your booking/My Tickets. Each ticket has its own private credential. Keep the QR and private download to yourself. A safe share preview omits admission credentials and private details. Check the live status rather than relying on an old screenshot.
8. **At the entrance.** Select the correct ticket, show the QR and present ID when the event requires it. Staff must obtain server confirmation. A used, cancelled, revoked or wrong-event ticket cannot admit again. Verified Event Passport attendance appears after authorized check-in, not after payment. Multiple tickets do not create duplicate attendance stamps for the same account/event.
9. **Cancel or request a refund.** Use the booking's available cancellation action or My bookings → Refund requests. Enter your own booking reference and reason. Eligibility follows the saved policy and state; used tickets and exceptional cases go to support. An approved refund request does not send money. After an organizer reports an external transfer, only acknowledge receipt if you actually received it. That acknowledgement is not independent bank verification.
10. **Get help and manage privacy.** My bookings → Support creates a private request and shows replies. Do not post a QR, receipt or full banking details in public. Notifications are in-app; there is no promised email/SMS/push delivery. In the full authorized application, Profile editing and Settings control profile, attendance/discovery and optional notifications. Attendance starts private. Public discovery needs applicable opt-ins and eligible event/account access; private guest lists remain private. Sign out when finished on a shared device.

More detail: [booking and payment](CUSTOMER_BOOKING_AND_PAYMENT.md), [refund procedure](REFUND_OPERATIONS.md), [entrance procedure](ENTRANCE_STAFF_GUIDE.md). Actual event terms, contact and operating owners still need approval in [OWNER_ACTION_REQUIRED.md](OWNER_ACTION_REQUIRED.md).
