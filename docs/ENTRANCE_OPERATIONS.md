# Entrance operations — local Phase 5 rehearsal

October 26, 2026. Fictional events and TEST tickets only. This guide does not authorize real-world admission, live sales or deployment.

1. **Log in.** Open `http://localhost:3001/organizer/login` with your assigned fictional organizer/staff account. The organizer grants access through Staff, choosing the event and existing username. Guest-list access, check-in, finance, registration review and location are independent permissions. Staff must sign in again after a permission change.
2. **Choose the event.** Open Entrance and check the selected event name and date. The saved link uses `/organizer?tab=Entrance&event=<event-id>`. Never switch events to make a mismatched ticket work. Entry is refused for inactive, ended, cancelled or suspended events.
3. **Read attendance.** Expected includes currently eligible unarrived tickets plus admitted tickets; pending registrations are excluded. Issued tickets, registrations, confirmed bookings and rejected attempts are separate. Rejected attempts cover the Phase 5 validation/admission endpoints. Counters refresh after actions, on return to the tab and every 30 seconds while visible. Use Refresh attendance before making a decision from an older screen.
4. **Scan.** Start camera, allow browser camera permission and use the back camera where available. Select another camera if necessary, then start it again. Torch appears only when supported. Keep the guest's QR flat and fully visible. The camera stops after a recognized code, when hidden and when leaving the entrance page.
5. **Verify the guest.** The server first displays the registered holder, tier, event and reference. Check attendee identification before admission whenever required. For the fictional Halloween rehearsal, check the name and 18+ requirement, then acknowledge the check. Do not photograph or upload identity documents.
6. **Confirm.** Select Confirm admission. Only “Admission confirmed” after the server transaction authorizes the software rehearsal. It records the staff identifier, timestamp, ticket, method and audit history. Attendance and eligible Passport badges update privately. Two admissions for one account/event still create one Passport event.
7. **Handle refusals.** An unknown, wrong-event, revoked, refunded or otherwise ineligible ticket cannot admit. “Already checked in” includes the original event-local time and reference: do not admit again. Refer disputes to the organizer; never reuse another guest's ticket or override the refusal.
8. **Manual fallback.** With guest-list and check-in permission, search by registered name, ticket reference or booking reference. Filter by arrival status or any configured tier. Select Review manual entry for the correct eligible ticket, verify ID as required and confirm. This uses the same atomic admission service and records MANUAL in the audit. Never paste a booking reference into the private-code field.
9. **Connection failure.** Pause entry; no offline authorization or automatic queued admission exists. An interrupted request may already have committed. Restore connectivity, refresh the guest list and validate the same ticket again before taking further action. A duplicate response is evidence to investigate, not permission to admit twice. Escalate a prolonged outage to the organizer. Any paper/CSV operating fallback needs its own approved custody, reconciliation and dispute procedure; it is not authorized by this software guide.
10. **Close the entrance.** Stop camera, sign out and revoke temporary staff assignments when finished. Owners review recent admissions and staff activity. Use the existing authorized lifecycle process to close/cancel an event; do not delete tickets/history or simulate refunds. Ended events cannot admit under the existing policy.

## Private exports

Only the event owner or administrator can download the server-generated guest CSV. It includes holder, tier, ticket reference/status, arrival time and method; no email, receipt, registration answers, private location or QR token. Exports are audited and formula cells are escaped, including leading whitespace. Store privately, share only with separately authorized operators and delete under the approved retention policy. The file is a snapshot and cannot prove current eligibility or prevent duplicates offline.

Customers use My Tickets to open individual owned tickets, expand the QR or download a private SVG. A safe preview omits credentials, holder, ticket reference and private location/artwork. Keep the actual ticket private. Used/ineligible tickets show status without a functional QR.
