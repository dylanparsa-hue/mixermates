## Direction contract

MixerMates helps someone on a phone choose a Malaysian night out, connect with consenting adults and complete an approval-driven booking. Its distinctive mechanism is one night joining discovery, personal tickets and verified attendance. The fixed identity is the official M/wordmark, purple–pink brand, midnight navy and pale lavender themes. The owner explicitly selected the reference blend and authorized implementation without another design approval gate; this is a code-led application correction.

Palette: midnight #101632 for photographs, canvas #090B18 / #FAFAFF, ink #FAFAFF / #17172B, surface #101632 / #F5F1FF, accessible purple #BBA2FF / #6525CA, original purple/pink action gradient. Manrope carries headings and all reading/controls; the original Oxanium asset remains available for restrained brand use, rather than every heading. No font was added and no new font licensing or download is required.

Composition: full photographic opening with a readable two-line brand promise; one actual upcoming-event link at the lower edge; quiet location rail; functional discovery controls; large two-column event photography; a separate Halloween composition blended into navy; a community invitation paired with a photographic organizer invitation. At phone widths, event imagery and invitations stack, social tabs form a 2×2 group above search, and map/list is an explicit switch. Work surfaces retain clear forms, state and independent actions.
