# Organizer guide

Phase 11 · fictional localhost only. Use an authorized organizer account at `/organizer/login`. Roles and event ownership are enforced on the server. A scanner account does not become a financial reviewer. No live event, payment collection or public release is authorized.

1. **Create or resume a draft.** Open My events → Create event. The seven steps store event information, images, schedule/location, access, ticket tiers, registration questions, entry options/payment settings and the final review. Save draft, reload and reopen to continue. Configure categories, age and privacy explicitly. Unknown real event details must stay unconfirmed. Never treat fictional Halloween values as approved operating facts.
2. **Configure tiers.** Enter integer MYR prices, finite capacities, order limits and optional sale windows. Early Bird and VIP are ordinary admission tiers; VIP table reservations do not exist. Once bookings exist, edits must preserve purchased terms and inventory. Use the existing reviewed change workflow when direct edits are refused.
3. **Submit for review.** Check the saved preview and submit the draft. An independent administrator reviews publication. A published fictional event still does not enable real sales. Read correction/rejection notes before resubmitting. You cannot approve your own verification or payment recipient.
4. **Set payment instructions.** Open Payment methods. Upload your private supported QR image and recipient information, instructions and reference. Wait for independent review. A replacement creates a new pending version and retains history; it cannot silently replace the approved booking snapshot. Assign only an approved active destination to an owned event. No actual banking details belong in these test fixtures.
5. **Invite a guest.** In Booking reviews, select the correct owned event and generate an invitation for an existing account username. Keep the one-time secret private. Expiry, identity binding and revocation apply. The application does not send an external invitation automatically; this phase sends no messages. Revoking an invitation does not silently erase paid booking history.
6. **Review registrations and evidence.** Check private required answers and payment/BYOB proof in Booking reviews. Registration approval, BYOB review and independent funds confirmation are separate decisions. A screenshot is not a funds check. In a future authorized operation, match the incoming transaction against the exact original recipient and amount, record meaningful notes and attest the independent check. Here use fictional records only. Do not reject a guest merely because evidence is similar to another submission; shared/group evidence needs investigation.
7. **Reconcile money.** Open financial reconciliation and its filters/history. Gross organizer-received funds, pending claims, refund obligations, reported transfers and customer acknowledgements are different measures. Organizer funds are not MixerMates revenue. Exports are private, formula-safe and scoped; protect downloaded files. A streaming export is not a frozen accounting snapshot.
8. **Handle a missing ticket.** Refresh saved booking status. If funds are already confirmed, do not request payment again. Use the eligible issuance retry or ask the administrator to recover issuance. Current event, invitation/registration/BYOB/funds rules still apply. Recovery cannot revive a revoked ticket.
9. **Manage guests and staff.** Guest list supports server search, filters, pages and scoped CSV. Assign existing active staff accounts to the correct event, granting only the needed guest/check-in/registration/finance/location permissions. Revocation invalidates stale access; staff may need to sign in again after a permission change. Rehearse the door with the [staff guide](ENTRANCE_STAFF_GUIDE.md). Attendance analytics reflect saved check-ins, not purchases alone.
10. **Review readiness.** Event operations → Readiness checklist separates technical configuration, identity/payment/event/entrance/operations evidence and sales authorization. A green technical field does not verify a venue, bank account, phone or legal procedure. No readiness control activates sales.
11. **Change or cancel an event.** Request event change with explicit proposed local dates/times, reason and customer message. An independent administrator reviews it. Pending changes block affected operations; old booking terms remain saved. My events → Cancel event shows booking, pending-registration, active-ticket and confirmed direct-payment impact. Expand it and confirm only for the intended event. Cancellation blocks admission, preserves history and requires separate refund review; no transfer is automatic.
12. **Refunds, notifications and support.** Review eligible requests, record notes, progress through the permitted states and report a fictional transfer only in this environment. Customers acknowledge separately. Read in-app notifications; use private support for disputes. No real email, bank transfer, payout, commission deduction or escrow service is connected.

[Owner decisions](OWNER_ACTION_REQUIRED.md) must be resolved before real operations. [Refunds](REFUND_OPERATIONS.md), [event changes](EVENT_RESCHEDULING.md), [reconciliation](PAYMENT_RECONCILIATION.md) and [operations handover](OPERATIONS_HANDOVER.md) provide detailed procedures.
