# Organizer operations — Phase 6

Local fictional rehearsal only. Open http://localhost:3021/organizer/login and sign in with an isolated organizer account. Real sales remain disabled.

1. **Overview** shows your database counts and organizer-direct funds. Counts include your owned events, not another organizer's events. Historical confirmed receipts include payments subsequently referred for refund review; they are not platform revenue. Tier figures distinguish confirmed quantities from inventory reservations.
2. **My events** lets you search/filter owned drafts, pending, published, suspended, cancelled and completed events. Changes requested is shown from the existing review outcome. Use **Edit & manage** for tiers/form settings, **Preview** for the protected event view, and **Submit for review** for a ready draft. Publication does not enable sales.
3. **Booking reviews** or **Registrations** opens the existing event review workspace. Select an event. Review authorized answers, recipient snapshot, private proof, review history and notes. Payment confirmation needs independent funds verification and the expected amount. Registration approval and BYOB review are separate actions. Use issuer retry only after its requirements pass. A screenshot alone never confirms money.
4. **Payments** shows expected outstanding amounts, review workload and confirmed historical organizer-direct amounts. **Payment methods** retains active, pending and previous recipient versions, images and review notes. Replacing a QR never changes an earlier booking snapshot. No gateway, payout, fee or escrow is added.
5. **Staff**, **Guest list** and **Entrance** retain Phase 5 event-specific permissions, private export, camera/manual checks, arrival rate, tier breakdown and audit. Scanner staff cannot approve payments. Physical phone/Safari/lighting/network rehearsal is still required.
6. In **My events**, expand **Cancel event**. Read the booking/pending/ticket counts and confirmed-payment obligations before **Confirm cancellation**. Cancellation blocks booking/issuance/admission, preserves history, sends in-app updates and marks confirmed money for refund review. It never returns money automatically. Use Event operations → Request event change for independently reviewed date/venue/entry changes; pending changes freeze sales/issuance/entry. See EVENT_RESCHEDULING.md.
7. **Refunds** shows the latest 100 organizer-direct requests for your events. Open the status history. Choose Under review, Approved or Rejected with notes. Approval cancels the booking and revokes its active tickets; this is confirmed before saving. Move approved requests to Awaiting organizer transfer. After a separate actual transfer, record its evidence reference as Transfer reported. Only the customer can independently confirm receipt. A reference is not verified banking evidence, and software cannot establish bank settlement.
8. **Notifications** lists your own latest 100 updates, ten per page. Open an update, mark it read or unread. **Settings** stores optional communication preferences; essential service updates remain available. No external email is configured. The explicit local job runner creates consented upcoming reminders; no production scheduler is configured. Refresh to update another device's changes.
9. **Support** lets you open and follow your own private case. Customer complaints, safety reports and disputes go to admins; being the event owner does not grant access to private complaints about you. Private/safety complaints stay admin-only. Routine booking/payment/ticket/cancellation cases create a generic organizer workload notice without reporter details.

Demo/legacy simulated refund records are preserved and are outside the organizer-direct request queue. Refund terms, real recipient/organizer checks, support ownership and retention still need approved operating policies. Do not use fictional confirmations for real payments.
